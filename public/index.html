How PathPilot works

A simple loop designed for consistency and reflection.

1

Commit to a path

Complete a tailored onboarding. Define your ultimate goal (e.g. Placements), pick a focus (e.g. DSA), and state your confidence. We assign you an archetype based on millions of data points.

2

Check in daily

Log your progress each day. Track your hours, your mood, and rate your friction points. Watch your heatmap fill up over a continuous 8-week period.

3

Review and adapt

Access your dashboard to observe your actual consistency rate versus your perceived effort. Use data to decide if it's time to pivot or stay the course.
